Privacy Policy

In short: What to Cook? works completely offline and does not collect, store, or share personal data.

Information we collect

What to Cook? does not collect personal information or user data. The app does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

Ingredient selections, recipe matching, recipe details, and cooking instructions are processed locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to Dimaso or to third parties.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.
